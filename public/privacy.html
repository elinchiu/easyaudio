Easy Audio 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

1. 關於本服務

Easy Audio（https://elinchiu.github.io/easyaudio/）是一個在瀏覽器中執行的音檔播放工具，讓你播放 Google Drive 資料夾裡的音檔。本服務沒有自己的伺服器或資料庫。

2. 我們存取的資料

若你只播放「知道連結的任何人都可檢視」的公開資料夾，不需要登入，我們不會存取你的 Google 帳號。

若你選擇「登入 Google」以讀取私人資料夾，本服務會要求以下權限：

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly：以唯讀方式查看你指定資料夾中的檔案名稱與音檔內容，只用於在你的瀏覽器中列出並播放音檔。

本服務不會修改、刪除或上傳你 Google Drive 中的任何檔案。

3. 資料如何被儲存

  • Google 存取權杖（access token）：只保存在瀏覽器記憶體中，重新整理或關閉頁面後即消失，不會傳送到我們的伺服器，因為我們沒有伺服器。
  • 瀏覽器本機儲存：為了加快使用體驗，音檔時長等少量設定，以及為了加快播放而快取的音檔資料，可能會儲存在你自己裝置的瀏覽器中。這些資料不會離開你的裝置，你可以隨時在瀏覽器設定中清除網站資料。

4. 我們不會做的事

  • 不蒐集、不儲存、不出售你的個人資料。
  • 不將你的 Google 使用者資料分享給任何第三方。
  • 不將你的資料用於廣告或使用者分析。

5. Google API 服務使用者資料政策

Easy Audio 對從 Google API 取得的資訊之使用與轉移，將遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包括「有限使用」（Limited Use）規範。

Easy Audio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. 撤銷權限

你可以隨時到 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權限頁面，移除 Easy Audio 的存取權。

7. 政策變更

若本政策有更新，會在此頁面公告並更新上方日期。

8. 聯絡方式

若對本政策有任何問題，請來信：elinautumn@gmail.com

Easy Audio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. About the Service

Easy Audio (https://elinchiu.github.io/easyaudio/) is a browser-based audio player that lets you play audio files from your Google Drive folders. The Service has no server or database of its own.

2. Data We Access

If you only play public folders that are viewable by "anyone with the link," no sign-in is required and we do not access your Google account.

If you choose to "Sign in with Google" in order to read a private folder, the Service requests the following permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly: Read-only access to view the file names and audio content in the folders you specify. It is used only to list and play audio files in your browser.

The Service does not modify, delete, or upload any files in your Google Drive.

3. How Data Is Stored

  • Google access token: Kept only in your browser's memory and discarded when you refresh or close the page. It is never sent to our servers, because we do not have any.
  • Browser local storage: To make the experience faster, a small amount of settings (such as audio durations) and audio data cached for quicker playback may be stored in the browser on your own device. This data never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time through your browser's site data settings.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your Google user data with any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or user analytics.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Easy Audio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking Access

You can remove Easy Audio's access at any time on the third-party access page of your Google Account.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, we will post the changes on this page and update the date above.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please email us at elinautumn@gmail.com
